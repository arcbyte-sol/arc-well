# Arcwell Consulting — Niche Research

**Reference theme:** "Thrown" — Business Consulting Agency (Next.js), Envato Elements
**Niche:** Business / management consulting for small and mid-size companies
**Date:** 2026-11-25

## 1. What the theme offers
- 10 home pages, but only **5 real layouts** (the 5 dark homes are copies of the light ones).
- Pages: About, Services, Service details, Team, Pricing, Projects, Contact, Blog, 404.
- Style: Jost headings + Heebo body, one generic blue (`#0154F7`), lots of stock office photos.

### Best home page: **Home Two** (`/index-two`)
This one is built to convert. It's the only layout with all of these:
hero → about → **contact form high on the page** → services → why us → **work process** → **pricing** → team → testimonials → second contact → logos → blog.

### Weaknesses we fix
| Theme problem | Our fix |
|---|---|
| Generic stock photos of people in suits | No stock photos. UI-style visuals (charts, KPI cards, dashboards) built in code, which load fast and are license-free |
| Placeholder copy ("Tiling And Painting" listed as a consulting service, "Get A Quout", "Priching") | Real, outcome-focused copy written for consulting buyers |
| Same blue as thousands of templates | Our own palette: deep ink + lime accent with editorial serif headings, which stands out in the niche |
| Vague claims ("Best Digital Agency") | Specific numbers in case studies (what changed, by how much, how fast) |
| Pricing hidden on a subpage | Transparent pricing on the homepage, with a monthly/project toggle |
| 10 near-duplicate homes | One strong homepage plus the pages people actually use |

## 2. What buyers in this niche want (2026)
1. **Outcomes, not features.** Copy built around features instead of client results lowers conversion.
2. **Case studies with numbers.** Most B2B buyers read case studies while comparing vendors; named results with specific figures beat vague claims.
3. **Transparent pricing.** In Clutch's survey, cost information was the website element buyers rated most important (25%), just ahead of case studies and testimonials (23%).
4. **One clear next step.** Every section should lead to "Book a discovery call".
5. **Serve a long sales cycle.** Insights, a FAQ and a client portal let prospects come back without having to call.
6. **Fast and mobile-first.** Speed and responsive design are the top technical conversion factors.
7. **Controlled motion.** Subtle micro-interactions and scroll reveals, nothing gimmicky.

## 3. Site plan (what we built)
- **Home:** hero with live-looking KPI dashboard → client logos → results counters → services → process → case studies (filterable) → pricing (toggle) → testimonials → FAQ → booking widget + contact form → newsletter
- **About:** story, values, team (initial avatars), timeline
- **Contact:** form + office info + discovery call booking
- **Client Portal:** demo login → dashboard with project progress, KPIs and messages

All "backend" behavior is simulated in the browser (spinners, toasts, localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
